1. 📈 Sales Trend:
   Sales showed a consistent upward trend from June to December, with December being the highest-performing month across all categories.

2. 📍 Regional Performance:
   The West region generated the highest total sales, followed by the East. However, the Central region showed the highest profit margin relative to sales.

3. 🛒 Category Contribution:
   Technology was the top-selling product category, contributing over 35% to total sales, while Office Supplies had the lowest sales but maintained steady performance.

4. 💼 Sub-Category Spotlight:
   Phones and Chairs were the leading sub-categories in terms of sales. Phones dominated Technology, while Chairs led in Furniture.

5. 📉 Profit Analysis:
   Despite high sales in some sub-categories like Tables and Binders, their profit margins were relatively low or even negative, suggesting discounting or cost issues.

6. 🌍 Geographical View:
   California and New York were the top states in terms of both sales and profit, while states like Texas had high sales but lower profit margins.
